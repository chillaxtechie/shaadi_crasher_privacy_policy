1. Introduction

Chronicle Play ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game application Shaadi Crasher (the "App"). This Privacy Policy informs users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our App.

By downloading and playing Shaadi Crasher, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

Shaadi Crasher is built with an offline-first, privacy-focused architecture:

  • No User Account or Login: We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.
  • On-Device Local Database: All game progression, including coins collected, feast levels cleared, high scores, and unlocked disguise outfits, is stored strictly on your local device using Android Room (SQLite database). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any private server or cloud database.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide optional gameplay rewards (such as unlocking bonus disguises or reviving your feast score after being spotted), the App displays advertisements integrated via Google AdMob (Google Play Services Ads SDK).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable privacy laws, including:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID) to serve relevant advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and frequency-cap video ads.
  • Diagnostics & Performance Telemetry: Crash logs, ad viewability metrics, and SDK performance data.
  • IP Address & Coarse Location: Used by Google to serve region-appropriate ads.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimum essential permissions necessary for standard functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to request and display Google AdMob interstitial and rewarded video ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Declared in compliance with Google Play's policy for Android 13+ (API level 33+) to allow AdMob to access the advertising identifier for fraud detection and ad serving.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise GPS location, SMS, or storage photos.

5. Children's Privacy

Shaadi Crasher is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided personal information via third-party services, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to ensure its removal.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all gameplay records (coins, skins, scores) are saved locally on your physical device, you maintain full control over your data:

  • Clearing App Data: You can wipe all stored game progress at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Shaadi Crasher > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling: Completely uninstalling the App automatically deletes all associated local database files from your device.
  • Ad ID Reset: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings > Google > Ads.

7. Security

We value your trust and strive to protect your gameplay data using industry-standard Android sandbox isolation and secure ProGuard/R8 release minification.
